## Projects

For all up-to-date projects logs see the [blog](/blog/)

* [VFD for Induction Motors](/blog/completion-of-vfd-pcb-and-demo/): A variable frequency drive (VFD) designed and built for mid-power consumer induction motors. Capable of controlling speed and torque of single and three-phase motors.

* [FOC Driver for PMSMs](/blog/first-post-on-ac-servo-drive-project/): A motor driver for permanent magnet AC motors. It implements an FOC algorithm on an STM32 to control torque, speed, and position of the motor.

* [ARManda](https://hackaday.io/project/173330-3d-printable-robotic-arm-armanda): A 4 DOF robotic manipulator from scratch. It uses NEMA stepper motors for the actuators, described in another project, and runs on ROS (RViz and MoveIt).

* [BandoMap](https://github.com/mAzurkovic/BandoMap): A web app for FPV drone pilots to find local flying spots as suggested by other pilots.

* [NEMA Actuator](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4428749): A modular robotic actuator powered by a NEMA 17 stepper motor and custom planetary gearbox.

* [Custom CNC Machine](/blog/revised-cnc-mill-design/): A 3-axis CNC machine specified and designed to be able to mill Aluminium. All parts chosen and assembled in Solidworks.
